# Gastador

**Know where your money goes.** Gastador is a simple budget tracker built around paydays: it shows how much you are really free to spend this month after your bills, reminds you before anything is due, and keeps your records private.

## Why Gastador

Salary arrives, bills arrive right after it, and by the middle of the month it is hard to tell what is actually left. Gastador answers that with one number on the home screen:

> **Free to spend this month** = your balance + salary still to come − bills still due − possible expenses

Everything else in the app keeps that number honest: bills grouped by pay period, receipts read for you, charts of where the money went, and a wishlist that shows your balance *before* you buy.

## Install on Android

1. On your Android phone, tap **[Download gastador.apk](https://github.com/andreagecolea/gastador/raw/main/gastador.apk)**. The download starts straight away.
2. Open the downloaded file (from the notification, or the Downloads folder in your Files app).
3. If your phone says the browser is not allowed to install apps, tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.
5. Sign in with Google or with your email.

**Updating:** download the newest `gastador.apk` from this page and install it over the old one. Your data stays.

**"Play Protect" notice:** the app is not from the Play Store, so Android may ask for confirmation. Choose **Install anyway**.

## Add to an iPhone

There is no App Store download. Gastador runs as a Home Screen web app, full screen with its own icon.

1. Open **[gastadorph.netlify.app](https://gastadorph.netlify.app/)** in **Safari** (other iPhone browsers may not offer the next step).
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

The sign-in screen has the same instructions behind the Apple and Android buttons, each with a QR code you can scan from a computer.

## First-time setup

1. **Create an account.** Choose **Continue with Google**, or **Create account** to use an email and password. With email, open the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
2. **Tell Gastador about your pay.** Enter the name you want to be greeted with, your salary, and how you are paid: twice a month (for example the 1st and 16th), monthly, weekly, every two weeks, or daily.
3. **Add your starting balance** so the numbers match what you actually have.
4. **Allow notifications** if you want reminders before bills are due.

## Using the app

### Home

- The green card shows **Free to spend this month**, with the figures behind it underneath. Tap the small **i** for the explanation.
- Tap the **eye** beside an amount to hide every amount in the app. Tap again to show them.
- **Add income** and **Add expense** record money in and out. The round **+** button in the tab bar adds an expense from any screen.
- The **bell** lists alerts: bills due soon, overdue bills, and budget warnings.
- The **magnifier** searches expenses, bills, wishes and more from any screen.

### Adding an expense, and scanning receipts

1. Tap **Add expense**.
2. Pick the category and item, type the amount, and choose where it was paid from (salary balance, a card, or other money).
3. Tap **Save**.

To skip the typing, tap **Scan receipt** (camera) or **Upload** (a saved photo). While it scans you will see the photo with a moving scan line. When it finishes, the store, date, amount and category are filled in. Check them, then save.

Scanned receipts are kept under **More > Receipts**, with the photo and the items that were read.

### Bills and dues

- Tap **+** to add a bill: name, amount, and the day it is due.
- A bill can repeat **every month** or only in the **months you tick**.
- If the amount changes from month to month (electricity, for example), open the bill in that month and set that month's amount only.
- Mark something as a **Possible expense** when you are not sure it will happen. It is counted for the whole month so you are not caught short.
- Mark something as a **Salary deduction** when it is taken from your pay before you receive it.
- Tap **Pay** when you have paid a bill. Each pay period shows what is left after its bills, and **Overall** adds the periods together.

### Charts

See spending split into **needs, wants and savings** against your plan, by pay period or by month. A bar turns orange as it nears its limit and red once it passes it. Below are daily spending and your top categories.

### Wishlist

Add things you want to buy with their price. Each one shows what your free-to-spend amount would be afterwards, so you can decide before spending. Tap **Mark as bought** when you buy it and it becomes an expense.

### Other money

Money that is not your salary (a bonus, a loan, a gift) gets its own place, so it does not blur your salary budget. Open one to list **possible expenses** with prices while you compare options. Nothing is spent until you press **Buy**.

### Wallet

Add your debit and credit cards by nickname and last 4 digits only. For credit cards Gastador tracks what you owe and when payment is due.

### Coach

Open **Coach** for saving suggestions worked out from your own numbers, or ask a question about your budget. The coach only answers money questions.

### Settings

Under **More > Settings** you can change your salary and pay schedule, turn reminders on or off (including email reminders), set an **app lock** passcode, make an **encrypted backup**, restore one, or reset the app.

## Privacy and security

- **Your records are encrypted** before they are saved to your account, using a sync passcode only you know. Receipt photos are encrypted the same way.
- **App lock** encrypts the data stored on the device itself.
- **Backups are encrypted files**, not readable text.
- **The coach sees budget totals only.** Card details, names and other personal details are not sent.
- **Cards** are stored as a nickname and the last 4 digits. Full card numbers are never asked for.
- One account per email address.

If you forget your sync passcode, the encrypted copy in your account cannot be opened by anyone, including the developer. Keep it somewhere safe.

## Questions and fixes

**Android will not install the file.**
Open your phone's Settings, search for **Install unknown apps**, choose the browser or Files app you used, and switch on **Allow from this source**.

**I do not see "Add to Home Screen" on my iPhone.**
Use Safari. Scroll further down the share sheet; the option is below the row of app icons.

**My data is not on my other device.**
Sign in with the same account and enter the same sync passcode. Changes then sync automatically.

**I did not get the confirmation or password email.**
Check the spam folder, then try again after a minute.

**Amounts show as dots.**
Amounts are hidden. Tap the eye beside any amount.
